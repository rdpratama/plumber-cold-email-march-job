# The Plumber's Inbox: A 120-Word Cold Email

A 118-word cold email pitching bookkeeping to a busy plumber who runs a two-van firm.

Subject: **"The March job nobody paid for"**

Instead of opening with the usual "out the door at six" paperwork complaint, the email opens with the plumber's sharpest pain — money that hasn't landed: a burst pipe fixed in March, invoice sent, still unpaid. It then shows the week (early starts, emergency call-outs, paperwork at night), offers one concrete service at a plain price ($89 a month, flat, no contract, first month free), and ends with the lightest ask in the field: reply with the name of one customer who owes you, and that invoice gets chased first, free.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the email to `/output/entry.txt`.
